travel plans on the car

  1. Research and Route Mapping: Use tools like Google Maps or Roadtrippers to identify key stops and scenic detours.
  2. Set Daily Driving Limits: Aim for 5–6 hours of driving per day and build in a 25% time buffer for traffic.
  3. Vehicle Maintenance Check: Inspect oil levels, tire pressure (including the spare), brakes, and battery health.
  4. Assemble an Emergency Kit: Pack jumper cables, a flashlight, first aid supplies, and at least one gallon of water per person.
  5. Book Accommodations: Reserve hotels or campsites in advance, especially for high-traffic destinations like National Parks.
  6. Download Offline Content: Save offline maps via Maps.me or Google Maps and download music or podcasts for areas with no cell service.
  7. Final Document Review: Verify you have a valid driver’s license, vehicle registration, and proof of insurance before departing.
